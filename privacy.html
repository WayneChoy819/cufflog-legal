CuffLog Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how CuffLog (“we”) handles information when you use the CuffLog iOS app. CuffLog is a home cuff log. It does not measure blood pressure. It does not create an account and it does not operate a cloud service for your readings.

1. What we store on your iPhone

You may enter systolic, diastolic, pulse, arm, notes, timestamps, an appointment date, reminder times, and display preferences. These readings and settings are stored locally on your device. We do not receive a copy of this information. Face ID / passcode lock uses the system authenticator; we do not receive biometric data. Reminders are local notifications on the device.

2. Apple Health (optional)

If you import from Health, CuffLog may read blood pressure already stored in Apple Health. If you turn on “Write new readings to Apple Health” in Settings, new readings you log afterwards are also saved to Apple Health — off by default, and you can turn it off at any time. CuffLog does not talk to a cuff over Bluetooth, and does not use Health data for advertising, marketing, or data mining. Revoke access in iOS Settings → Health.

3. When information leaves the device

Only if you share a PDF or CSV, or if your iPhone backup copies app data. We do not operate those services.

4. Purchases

If you buy CuffLog Yearly or CuffLog Lifetime, payment is processed by Apple. We do not receive your full payment card number. Apple’s privacy policy applies to billing. We do not operate a CuffLog account for purchases.

5. What we do not do

No accounts, analytics SDKs, advertising, crash reporters, or CuffLog servers for readings. We do not sell your information. We do not use HealthKit data for marketing.

6. Children

CuffLog is not directed at children under 13.

7. Deletion

Delete readings in the app (including Delete all readings) or delete the app. Health data stays in Apple Health unless you delete it there.

8. Medical notice

CuffLog is not a medical device, not a diagnostic tool, and not a substitute for a licensed clinician. Color labels are published ACC/AHA or ESC/ESH ranges for reference only.
