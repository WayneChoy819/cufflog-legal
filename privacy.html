CuffLog Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how CuffLog (“CuffLog”, “we”) handles information when you use the CuffLog iOS app.

CuffLog is a home cuff log. It does not measure blood pressure. It does not create an account and it does not operate a cloud service for your readings.

1. What we store on your iPhone

You may enter systolic, diastolic, pulse, arm, notes, timestamps, an appointment date, reminder times, and display preferences. These readings and settings are stored locally on your device (including using Apple’s on-device storage). We do not receive a copy of this information.

If you turn on Face ID or passcode lock, CuffLog asks the system to authenticate you. We do not receive your biometric data.

If you turn on reminders, CuffLog schedules local notifications on your device. Those notifications are not sent to us.

2. Apple Health (optional)

If you choose “Import cuff readings from Health”, CuffLog requests permission to read blood pressure already stored in Apple Health (for example by your cuff’s app). If you turn on “Write new readings to Apple Health” in Settings, new readings you log afterwards are also saved to Apple Health, and you can copy earlier readings there with a button. This is off by default, and you can turn it off at any time; CuffLog never writes readings that came from Health back into Health. CuffLog does not talk to a cuff over Bluetooth, and does not use Health data for advertising, marketing, or data mining. You can revoke access in iOS Settings → Health. If you deny access, CuffLog still works as a manual log.

3. When information leaves the device

Readings leave the device only if you act: you preview and share a PDF or CSV, or iOS backup (such as encrypted iCloud Backup of the phone) copies app data as part of backing up the device. We do not operate the share sheet or Apple’s backup.

4. Purchases

If you buy CuffLog Monthly or CuffLog Lifetime, payment is processed by Apple. We do not receive your full payment card number. Apple’s privacy policy applies to that transaction. Purchase records stay with Apple. We do not operate a CuffLog account for billing.

5. What we do not do

We do not run accounts, analytics SDKs, advertising, crash reporters, or our own servers for CuffLog. We do not sell your information. We do not use HealthKit data for marketing.

6. Children

CuffLog is not directed at children under 13.

7. Retention and deletion

Data stays on your iPhone until you delete readings in the app or delete the app. Uninstalling CuffLog removes its local store. Apple Health data remains in Health unless you delete it there.

8. Medical notice

CuffLog is not a medical device, not a diagnostic tool, and not a substitute for a licensed clinician. Color labels are published AHA/ACC 2025 or ESH 2023 ranges for reference only.
